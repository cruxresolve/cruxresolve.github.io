Privacy Policy

Crux Resolve LLC  ·  GhostTune  ·  Effective Date: June 5, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Crux Resolve LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the GhostTune mobile application ("Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy applies to all users of the Application.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

GhostTune is designed to operate entirely on your local device and local network. We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials
  • Your ECU data, tune files, or engine parameters
  • Your vehicle information or location data
  • Any data generated during tuning sessions

All communication between GhostTune and your ECU occurs over your local WiFi network. No tuning data leaves your device.

3. Information That May Be Collected Automatically

The Application itself does not collect analytics or usage data. However, certain minimal technical information may be collected by the platforms through which the Application is distributed or operated:

  • Apple App Store: Apple may collect information related to your download and purchase of the Application under Apple's own Privacy Policy. We do not receive personally identifiable information from Apple beyond what is necessary to process a purchase.
  • Crash Reports: If you have enabled crash reporting on your iOS device, Apple may forward anonymized crash data to us to help identify and fix bugs. This data does not include your tuning data or personal information.

3a. Website and Support Communications

If you contact us by email, submit a support request, or express interest in beta access, we may receive information you choose to provide — such as your email address, message contents, device model, iOS version, ECU firmware version, screenshots, or hardware setup details. We use this information only to respond to your request, manage beta access, and improve GhostTune. We do not sell or share this information with third parties.

4. How We Use Information

Any information we receive (such as crash reports) is used solely for the purpose of improving the Application — diagnosing bugs, improving stability, and enhancing the user experience. We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any information we receive against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Because GhostTune does not transmit your tuning data or personal information over the internet, your ECU data remains on your device and local network at all times.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out: opt out of certain data collection or communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@cruxresolve.com.

8. Apple App Store

The Application is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect certain information in connection with your download and use of the Application pursuant to Apple's own Privacy Policy, available at apple.com/privacy. We encourage you to review Apple's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the effective date at the top of this Policy and, where appropriate, by posting a notice within the Application. Your continued use of the Application following any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

privacy@cruxresolve.com

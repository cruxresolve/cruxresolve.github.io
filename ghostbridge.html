// WHAT IT DOES

Simple idea.
Serious execution.

GhostBridge sits between your serial device and your phone. That's it. No laptop in the middle, no proprietary cables, no software to install.

🔌
Plug In and Go
DB9 into your device, USB-C for power. The bridge comes up broadcasting its own WiFi network — nothing to configure, nothing to install.
📡
Its Own WiFi Network
No router, no infrastructure needed. GhostBridge creates its own access point wherever you are — garage, dyno, field, job site.
🔒
Stays Local
Everything runs on the direct link between the bridge and your device. Nothing goes to the internet. Nothing is logged or transmitted anywhere.
🏗️
Built to Last
Custom-designed enclosure with debossed branding, rubber feet, and M3 screw retention. Not a bare PCB stuffed in a project box.
// SETUP

Three steps.

If you can plug in a cable and join a WiFi network, you're done.

01
Plug in
Connect the DB9 to your serial device and power the bridge via USB-C. It broadcasts its own WiFi network immediately — no boot sequence to wait for.
02
Connect
Join the bridge's WiFi network from your phone or tablet. Each unit has a unique network name so there's no confusion if multiple bridges are nearby.
03
Use your app
Open your application and connect via TCP to 192.168.4.1 on port 35000. The bridge transparently relays serial data in both directions. GhostTune does this automatically.

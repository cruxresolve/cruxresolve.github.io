// WHY GHOSTBRIDGE

Built for GhostTune.
Built for the real world.

Off-the-shelf WiFi adapters weren't designed for ECU serial protocols. GhostBridge is — tuned firmware, proper RS232 levels, and an enclosure built to survive a garage.

🔌
Plug and Play
DB9 straight into your ECU's diagnostic port. USB-C for power. No soldering, no breadboards, no configuration files to edit.
📡
Dedicated WiFi AP
Broadcasts its own access point (GhostTune-WiFi). No router needed. Connect your iPhone directly — even in a field or on a dyno.
🧠
GhostTune Firmware
Flashed with firmware written specifically for GhostTune's communication protocol. Not a generic serial bridge — every timing decision was made with MS2/Extra in mind.
🏗️
Custom Enclosure
PLA enclosure designed from scratch for this exact PCB stack. Rev 12.0 with reinforced DB9 closure, anti-flex ribs, M3 screw retention, rubber feet, and a debossed ghost logo on the lid.
🔒
Local Network Only
All ECU data stays on the local WiFi link between the bridge and your phone. Nothing routed to the internet. Nothing logged to a server.
⚡
MAX3232 RS232
Proper RS232 voltage levels via ANMBEST MAX3232 module. No level-shifting hacks. Reliable signal integrity at the baud rates MS2/Extra expects.

// SETUP

Up and running in minutes.

No drivers. No laptop. No configuration wizard.

01
Connect DB9 to your ECU
Plug the GhostBridge DB9 connector into your ECU's RS232 diagnostic port — the same port TunerStudio* uses. Finger-tight is fine.
02
Power via USB-C
Connect a USB-C cable from any 5V source — a USB charger, your car's USB port, or a small power bank. The bridge powers up and begins broadcasting immediately.
03
Join GhostTune-WiFi on your iPhone
Open iPhone Settings → Wi-Fi → select GhostTune-WiFi. No password required. The bridge is its own access point — no router needed.
04
Open GhostTune and connect
Launch GhostTune, tap Connect. The app finds the bridge at 192.168.4.1:35000 automatically. Live data starts flowing within seconds.
05
Tune
Edit VE tables, monitor live gauges, review diffs, and burn to ECU — all from your iPhone. No laptop. No cable to your phone. No compromise.

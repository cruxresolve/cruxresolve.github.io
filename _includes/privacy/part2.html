7. Information voluntarily provided

When a person contacts Crux Resolve LLC for support, feedback, compatibility review, testing, privacy, or another inquiry, the Company may receive the information deliberately included in that communication. This can include contact details, device and operating-system information, ECU and firmware information, screenshots, logs, tune files, diagnostic descriptions, and other attachments.

Do not submit credentials, private keys, complete payment-card information, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for the request.

8. Apple services and diagnostic information

Apple processes information relating to App Store downloads, purchases, device operation, and Apple services under Apple’s own privacy policies. If a user enables Apple’s option to share crash or diagnostic information with developers, Apple may make limited diagnostic information available to Crux Resolve LLC. The Company uses it only for stability, safety, security, accessibility, and support.

9. How information is used

  • Responding to support, compatibility, privacy, and product inquiries.
  • Sending an update specifically requested through an optional form.
  • Measuring website traffic and improving site content when analytics consent is granted.
  • Accepting, fulfilling, supporting, and documenting GhostBridge orders.
  • Investigating technical problems and improving stability, safety, accessibility, and functionality.
  • Preventing fraud, misuse, and security threats.
  • Maintaining appropriate support, tax, accounting, warranty, and business records.
  • Complying with legal obligations and valid legal process.

Crux Resolve LLC does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising or cross-application tracking.

10. Disclosure and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers used for website hosting, optional analytics, email, forms, payment processing, shipping, file storage, and support. This includes Google when a visitor allows website analytics. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary for legal compliance, safety, fraud prevention, a business transaction, or at the person’s direction.

11. Retention, deletion, and choices

Local GhostTune files remain on the user’s device until deleted, removed with the application, or handled through an applicable in-app function. Notification-list information, support communications, order records, analytics reports, and related attachments are retained only as reasonably necessary for the requested service, business records, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

Website visitors can change their analytics preference through Privacy choices in the footer. Requests for access, correction, deletion, or removal from a notification list may be sent to privacy@cruxresolve.com. Some information may be retained when required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

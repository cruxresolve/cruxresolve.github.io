Scope: This Policy covers the GhostTune application, the Crux Resolve website, optional testing and product-update forms, product checkout information received from Stripe, and information voluntarily submitted for support.

1. Local operation of GhostTune

GhostTune is designed to operate locally on an Apple device and over a local WiFi connection between that device and a compatible ECU or ECU WiFi bridge. The current version does not require or create a user account and does not use a Crux Resolve cloud service to operate its ECU tuning features.

  • GhostTune does not contain advertising or cross-application tracking technology.
  • GhostTune does not use developer-controlled analytics or remote telemetry.
  • GhostTune does not automatically transmit ECU data, tune files, engine parameters, sensor readings, logs, or tuning-session information to Crux Resolve LLC.
  • ECU information, tune data, settings, and files created by GhostTune are processed or stored locally unless the user deliberately exports or shares them.

2. Local files and user-directed sharing

GhostTune may store settings, tune backups, logs, reports, and other files in the application’s private storage. When a user chooses to import, export, save, or share a file, GhostTune may use Apple’s document picker, Files interface, or share sheet. Crux Resolve LLC receives a file only when the user deliberately sends it to the Company, such as as an attachment to a support request.

3. Website hosting and routine request data

The website is hosted through third-party infrastructure. Hosting, network, and security providers may process routine request information—such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time—as necessary to deliver and protect the website. Crux Resolve LLC does not operate a behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking system on this website.

The public website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. A third-party service opened by the user, including Stripe or Formspree, may use its own cookies or similar technology under its own privacy practices.

4. Optional forms and Formspree

The GhostTune testing-and-release form and GhostBridge stock-update form are optional. Submissions are processed by Formspree. The GhostTune form requires an email address and interest selection and may include optional ECU hardware, firmware version, vehicle or application, device model, tuning experience, and notes. The GhostBridge form requests an email address. Formspree may also process the selected form, submission time, and routine technical information needed to provide the service.

Crux Resolve LLC uses submitted information only for the requested testing, product update, compatibility review, or related administrative communication. The Company does not sell the information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. A person may request removal by emailing privacy@cruxresolve.com.

5. GhostBridge checkout and Stripe

GhostBridge checkout is provided by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes payment-card details under Stripe’s own privacy and security practices. Crux Resolve LLC does not receive or store the complete payment-card number.

Crux Resolve LLC may receive information needed to accept and fulfill an order, such as the buyer’s name, email address, shipping address, order details, payment status, tax information, and transaction identifiers. This information is used for payment administration, fraud prevention, fulfillment, support, tax records, disputes, and legal compliance.

Scope: This Policy covers the GhostTune application, the Crux Resolve website, optional website analytics, optional testing and product-update forms, product checkout information received from Stripe, and information voluntarily submitted for support.

1. Local operation of GhostTune

GhostTune is designed to operate locally on an Apple device and over a local WiFi connection between that device and a compatible ECU or ECU WiFi bridge. The current version does not require or create a user account and does not use a Crux Resolve cloud service to operate its ECU tuning features.

  • GhostTune does not contain advertising or cross-application tracking technology.
  • GhostTune does not use developer-controlled analytics or remote telemetry.
  • GhostTune does not automatically transmit ECU data, tune files, engine parameters, sensor readings, logs, or tuning-session information to Crux Resolve LLC.
  • ECU information, tune data, settings, and files created by GhostTune are processed or stored locally unless the user deliberately exports or shares them.

2. Local files and user-directed sharing

GhostTune may store settings, tune backups, logs, reports, and other files in the application’s private storage. Crux Resolve LLC receives a file only when the user deliberately sends it to the Company, such as with a support request.

3. Website hosting and routine request data

The website is hosted through third-party infrastructure. Hosting, network, and security providers may process routine request information, such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and request time, to deliver and protect the website. Crux Resolve LLC does not operate behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking on this website.

4. Optional Google Analytics

The website offers optional Google Analytics 4 measurement to help Crux Resolve LLC understand how visitors find and use the site. The Google Analytics tag is not loaded unless a visitor selects Allow analytics. Declining does not prevent use of the website.

When allowed, Google Analytics may process pages viewed, page titles, referring sources, approximate location, browser and device characteristics, interaction events, and identifiers stored in first-party analytics cookies. This information is transmitted to Google for analytics processing. Google signals and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in the website configuration, and Crux Resolve LLC does not use analytics for targeted advertising.

The analytics choice is stored in the browser’s local storage. It can be reviewed or changed through Privacy choices in the website footer. Declining after previously allowing analytics prevents the tag from loading on later page views and attempts to remove accessible Google Analytics cookies.

5. Optional forms and Formspree

The GhostTune testing-and-release form and GhostBridge stock-update form are optional and processed by Formspree. The forms may collect an email address, interest selection, ECU and firmware details, vehicle or application, device model, tuning experience, notes, submission time, and routine technical information needed to provide the service.

Crux Resolve LLC uses submitted information only for the requested testing, product update, compatibility review, or related administration. The Company does not sell the information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Removal requests may be sent to privacy@cruxresolve.com.

6. GhostBridge checkout and Stripe

GhostBridge checkout is provided by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes payment-card details under its own privacy and security practices. Crux Resolve LLC does not receive or store the complete payment-card number.

Crux Resolve LLC may receive information needed to accept and fulfill an order, including the buyer’s name, email address, shipping address, order details, payment status, tax information, and transaction identifiers.

// What it does

A transparent local bridge.

GhostBridge relays bytes between a local TCP connection and its RS232 interface. It is designed around GhostTune’s verified MicroSquirt workflow rather than broad, untested claims about every serial device.

🔌 Assembled hardware

The bridge arrives assembled for the published GhostTune setup. Connect the DB9 interface, provide USB-C power, and join its WiFi network.

📡 Its own WiFi network

No router is required. The iPhone or iPad connects directly to the bridge’s local access point.

🔐 Local communication

ECU traffic stays on the direct local connection. GhostBridge does not provide a cloud relay or remote logging service.

🧰 Rev 12 enclosure

The production enclosure uses a 3 mm main wall, reinforced connector closures, four M3 screw rails, internal ribs, rubber-foot recesses, and debossed branding.

// Setup

Three steps.

Connect and power

Connect the DB9 interface to the supported ECU serial connection and power GhostBridge through USB-C.

Join the bridge network

Choose the GhostBridge WiFi network from the iPhone or iPad WiFi settings.

Open GhostTune

GhostTune connects to 192.168.4.1 on TCP port 35000 and verifies the supported ECU profile.

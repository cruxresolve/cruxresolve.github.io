Privacy Policy

Crux Resolve LLC · Series GhostTune · Effective Date: June 23, 2026 · Version 3.0

1. HOW GHOSTTUNE HANDLES DATA

GhostTune is designed to operate locally on your Apple device and over a local WiFi connection between your device and a compatible ECU or ECU WiFi bridge. The current version of GhostTune does not require or create a user account and does not use a Company-controlled cloud service to operate its ECU tuning features.

  • GhostTune does not contain advertising or cross-application tracking technology.
  • GhostTune does not use a developer-controlled analytics or remote telemetry service.
  • GhostTune does not automatically transmit ECU data, tune files, engine parameters, sensor readings, logs, or tuning-session information to the Company.
  • GhostTune does not collect precise location, contacts, photos, payment-card details, advertising identifiers, vehicle identification numbers, or license-plate information.
  • ECU information, tune data, application settings, and files created by GhostTune are processed or stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

2. LOCAL FILES AND USER-DIRECTED SHARING

GhostTune may store settings, tune backups, logs, reports, and other application files in the Application's private storage on your device. Those local files remain under your control and are not automatically uploaded to the Company.

When you choose to import, export, save, or share a file, GhostTune may use Apple's document picker, Files interface, or share sheet. You select the file source and the destination. The Company does not receive a file merely because you export or share it. We receive a file only when you intentionally send it to us, such as by attaching it to a support request.

Information shared with another person, application, cloud-storage provider, email provider, or other destination selected by you is subject to that recipient's privacy and security practices.

3. INFORMATION YOU VOLUNTARILY PROVIDE

When you contact us for support, provide feedback, participate in testing, or otherwise communicate with us, we may receive information that you choose to provide.

  • Your name, email address, and other contact information included in your message.
  • The contents of your support request, feedback, or other communication.
  • Your device model, operating-system version, ECU type, firmware version, or bridge configuration when you provide those details for troubleshooting.
  • Screenshots, diagnostic descriptions, logs, tune files, configuration files, or other attachments that you deliberately submit.
  • Other information you choose to include in your communication.
  • Please do not submit credentials, private keys, financial information, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for us to respond to your request.

4. APPLE SERVICES AND DIAGNOSTIC INFORMATION

Apple processes information relating to App Store downloads, purchases, device operation, and use of Apple services under Apple's own privacy policies. Apple, not the Company, handles App Store payment information.

If you enable Apple's option to share analytics or crash information with application developers, Apple may make limited crash or diagnostic information available to us. We use any such information only to identify errors, improve stability, maintain security, and support the Application. GhostTune does not independently collect or transmit that diagnostic information through a Company-controlled analytics service.

Additional information about Apple's privacy practices is available at apple.com/privacy.

5. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We may use information voluntarily provided to us or made available through Apple for the following purposes:

  • Responding to support requests and feedback.
  • Investigating technical problems and compatibility concerns.
  • Improving the stability, safety, accessibility, and functionality of GhostTune.
  • Maintaining appropriate support, security, tax, and business records.
  • Protecting the Company, our users, and others from fraud, misuse, security threats, or unlawful activity.
  • Complying with legal obligations and valid legal process.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for targeted advertising or cross-application tracking.

6. HOW INFORMATION MAY BE DISCLOSED

  • Service Providers. We may use service providers to operate our business email, website, file storage, or support systems. They may process information only as reasonably necessary to provide those services. We require service providers that receive personal information from us to protect it consistently with this Policy and applicable law.
  • Legal and Safety Requirements. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, court order, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, safety, or security of the Company, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transactions. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, reorganization, financing, acquisition, sale of assets, or similar business transaction. Any recipient will be required to handle personal information consistently with this Policy or provide any notice or choices required by law.
  • At Your Direction. We may disclose information to a person or service when you direct or authorize us to do so, including when you deliberately send a file or communication to a selected recipient.

7. RETENTION, DELETION, AND YOUR CHOICES

Files stored locally by GhostTune remain on your device until you delete them, remove the Application, or use an applicable in-app deletion function.

Information submitted to Company support is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to address the request, maintain appropriate business and security records, protect legal rights, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable law. Attachments containing ECU, tune, or diagnostic information will be deleted or de-identified when they are no longer reasonably needed for those purposes.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that you voluntarily submitted to us by contacting us using the information below. We may retain information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Because the current version of GhostTune does not conduct developer-controlled analytics or tracking, there is no in-app analytics consent to withdraw. You may manage Apple diagnostic-sharing options through your device settings and may stop providing information to Company support at any time.

8. SECURITY

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information held by the Company from unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We also dispose of personal information in a manner intended to make it unreadable or unusable when it is no longer required.

No storage or communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for reviewing the privacy and security practices of any destination to which you choose to export or share a file.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

GhostTune is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or legal guardian may contact us regarding such information.

10. PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you reside, applicable law may provide rights concerning personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how it has been handled. We do not sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use personal information for targeted advertising.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and may take reasonable steps to verify the request and protect against fraudulent or unauthorized requests.

11. INTERNATIONAL COMMUNICATIONS

The Company is located in Illinois, United States. If you contact us from outside the United States, the information you submit may be processed and stored in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. We will handle the information as described in this Policy and as required by applicable law.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to GhostTune, our business practices, or applicable law. When we make changes, we will update the effective date and make the revised Policy publicly available. When appropriate, we may also provide notice within the Application. When applicable law requires consent to a new data practice, we will request that consent before beginning the practice.

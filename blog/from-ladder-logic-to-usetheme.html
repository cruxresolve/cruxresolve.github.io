Crux Resolve product origin story

This earlier behind-the-scenes article has been retired from the public technical library so current customer information remains focused on released products, supported configurations, setup, and use.

Why Crux Resolve exists

Crux Resolve grew from a practical need for better ways to connect mobile devices with vehicle and serial-connected systems. That work became two separate products: GhostTune for mobile ECU tuning and GhostBridge for local wireless serial communication.

The current website focuses on what those products do today, which configurations are verified, how to set them up, and where to get direct support.

// Our approach

Purpose-built tools. Clear boundaries. Practical support.

Crux Resolve is the parent engineering brand. GhostBridge serves serial connectivity, while GhostTune serves supported ECU tuning workflows.

Practical engineering

Products are built around real bench, garage, embedded, industrial, and serial-connected workflows rather than features added only for a specification sheet.

Local control

Direct support

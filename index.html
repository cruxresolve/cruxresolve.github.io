// Capabilities

Everything you need. On your phone.

[ 01 ]

VE Table Editing

Read, edit, and burn your volumetric efficiency table directly from your device. Live RPM and TPS overlays show exactly where you are in the map.

[ 02 ]

Live Data Gauges

Real-time engine data at a glance — AFR, MAP, RPM, coolant temp, TPS, and more. Configurable gauge screens built for the tuning bay.

[ 03 ]

Ignition & AFR Tables

Full read/write access to ignition timing and AFR target tables. Safe burn sequence with verify pass on every write.

[ 04 ]

Tune Manager

Save, load, and manage complete tune files in GhostTune's native .gtune format. Full 7-page ECU snapshots with instant restore.

[ 05 ]

Cranking & Idle

Dedicated screens for cranking enrichment, rev limiter, and idle control — the parameters you actually need at startup.

[ 06 ]

Comms Diagnostics

Built-in connection diagnostics with RTT stats, rolling protocol log, and live value verification. Know exactly what your ECU is saying.

// Compatibility

Built for MegaSquirt MS2/Extra

GhostTune is purpose-built for the MS2/Extra 3.4.x firmware platform — the most widely used aftermarket EFI system for enthusiast builds.

Connects wirelessly via an ESP32 WiFi bridge to your existing MS2 serial port. No hardware modifications to your ECU required.

  • MicroSquirt / MicroSquirt AMP'd
  • MegaSquirt 2 (MS2/Extra 3.4.x)
  • ESP32 WiFi Bridge (NodeMCU ESP-32S)
  • iPhone & iPad — iOS 15+

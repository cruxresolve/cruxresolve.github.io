// How we build

Purpose-built tools. Clear boundaries. Practical support.

The Crux Resolve name sits above the individual products. That gives each product room to solve its own problem while keeping a consistent engineering approach across software and hardware.

Practical engineering

Products are developed around real vehicle, bench, garage, and serial-connected workflows rather than features added only for a specification sheet.

Local control

GhostBridge keeps its working connection local, while GhostTune is designed around direct interaction with supported ECU hardware and explicit write safeguards.

Direct support

Questions about compatibility, ordering, setup, privacy, and support are handled directly through Crux Resolve.

// About Crux Resolve

A veteran-owned small business building connected tools.

Crux Resolve LLC is an Illinois veteran-owned small business building professional-grade mobile tuning software and wireless interface hardware for vehicle, embedded, and serial-connected systems.

The company is growing beyond a single application or bridge. Crux Resolve is the parent brand; GhostTune and GhostBridge are product families beneath it.

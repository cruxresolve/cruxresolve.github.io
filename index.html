// Our approach

Purpose-built tools. Clear boundaries. Practical support.

The Crux Resolve name sits above the individual products. That gives each product room to solve its own problem while keeping a consistent approach across software and hardware.

Practical engineering

Products are built around real vehicle, bench, garage, and serial-connected workflows rather than features added only for a specification sheet.

Local control

GhostBridge keeps its working connection local, while GhostTune is designed around direct interaction with supported ECU hardware and explicit write safeguards.

Direct support

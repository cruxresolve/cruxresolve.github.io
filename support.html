// Frequently asked questions

Common answers

What ECUs does GhostTune support?

The current release is verified with MicroSquirt hardware running MS2/Extra 3.4.4. Other platforms remain blocked unless explicitly added to the independently verified compatibility table.

How does GhostTune connect to the ECU?

GhostTune connects over local WiFi through a compatible WiFi-to-RS232 bridge. GhostBridge GB-01 is the assembled hardware designed and verified for this workflow.

Is tune data sent to the cloud?

No. ECU communication and tune data stay on the device and local WiFi link unless the user deliberately exports or shares a file.

Is GhostTune available now?

Yes. GhostTune v1.0 is now available on the App Store.

Can GhostBridge be used without GhostTune?

Yes. GhostBridge is a transparent local TCP-to-RS232 byte relay and is not software-locked to GhostTune. Other serial applications may be compatible when their complete electrical, cabling, serial, and TCP-client requirements match. Unlisted configurations are not represented as verified.

Does GhostBridge require internet access?

No. GhostBridge creates its own local WiFi access point, so a compatible client can connect directly without a router or cloud service.

Does every DB9 RS232 device work with GhostBridge?

No automatic compatibility claim is made from the connector alone. Pinout, electrical signaling, serial configuration, timing expectations, and software behavior must all be compatible.

What GhostBridge configuration is verified?

The published verified configuration is GhostTune with MicroSquirt hardware running MS2/Extra 3.4.4. Other applications may work but are only represented as verified after explicit testing.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@cruxresolve.com with the device model, operating-system version, ECU hardware, firmware version, what you expected, and what occurred. Screenshots or a short screen recording can help.

Can tuning damage an engine or ECU?

Incorrect ECU configuration can damage equipment or create unsafe operation. Keep a known-good backup, verify the supported profile, and do not tune a running engine in an unsafe environment.

Can I explore GhostTune without an ECU?

Yes. Demo Mode provides simulated data and access to the app’s screens without live hardware.

Where can I buy GhostBridge?

GhostBridge GB-01 assembled units are available now for $89 through the GhostBridge product page, subject to current stock and the published fulfillment window.

Tuning safety: GhostTune is intended for technically capable users. Incorrect ECU changes can damage engines, ECUs, or related equipment. Maintain a known-good backup and use the application only in a safe environment.

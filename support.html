// Frequently Asked Questions

What ECUs does GhostTune support?

GhostTune is built for MegaSquirt MS2/Extra 3.4.x firmware. This includes MicroSquirt and MicroSquirt AMP'd boards running MS2/Extra. Support for additional platforms is planned for future releases.

How does GhostTune connect to my ECU?

GhostTune connects wirelessly via an ESP32 WiFi bridge (NodeMCU ESP-32S) attached to your ECU's serial port. No hardware modifications to the ECU are required.

Is my tune data sent to the cloud?

No. All ECU data is processed locally on your device. We do not transmit your tune files or engine parameters to any server. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

When will GhostTune be available on the App Store?

GhostTune is currently in beta testing. App Store release is coming soon. Contact us at support@cruxresolve.com if you're interested in beta access.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email support@cruxresolve.com with a description of the issue, your device model, iOS version, and ECU firmware version. Screenshots or screen recordings are always helpful.

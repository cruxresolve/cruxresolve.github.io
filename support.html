Frequently Asked Questions

What ECUs does GhostTune support?

GhostTune is currently verified on MicroSquirt* hardware running MS2/Extra* 3.4.4 firmware. Compatibility testing for additional ECUs and firmware versions is ongoing. Check the compatibility table on the home page for current status.

How does GhostTune connect to my ECU?

GhostTune connects wirelessly via GhostBridge — a compact WiFi-to-serial bridge that plugs into your ECU's DB9 RS232 diagnostic port and powers over USB-C. Your iPhone connects to GhostBridge's WiFi network directly, no router required. The in-app Setup Wizard walks you through the full process.

Is my tune data sent to the cloud?

No. All ECU communication and tune data stays entirely on your device and local network. GhostTune does not transmit your tune files or engine parameters to any server. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

When will GhostTune be available on the App Store?

GhostTune has been submitted to the App Store and is currently under review. Once approved, it will be available for download. Leave your email on the main page and we'll notify you the moment it goes live.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email support@cruxresolve.com with a description of the issue, your device model, iOS version, and ECU firmware version. Screenshots or screen recordings are always helpful and speed up the fix.

Can GhostTune damage my ECU or tune?

GhostTune includes a multi-stage write safety system that verifies every burn before returning control. That said, tuning carries inherent risk — always keep a known-good tune backup before making changes, and never tune a running engine in an unsafe environment.

Does GhostTune work without a connection to an ECU?

Yes — Demo Mode lets you explore every screen and gauge with simulated live data, so you can familiarise yourself with the app before you're connected to an ECU.

Where can I get a GhostBridge?

GhostBridge assembled units are in preparation. Visit the GhostBridge page to join the notification list — we'll reach out directly when units are ready to order.

A Note on Tuning Safety

GhostTune is intended for experienced tuners. Incorrect ECU changes can damage engines or create unsafe operating conditions. Always maintain a known-good tune backup before making changes, and never tune a running engine in an unsafe environment.

## Naming standard
- utm_source: platform or referring site, lowercase
- utm_medium: referral, social, video, email, or cpc
- utm_campaign: stable campaign name, lowercase with underscores
- utm_content: optional placement/account/creative identifier

## Apple App Store campaign links

Create Apple campaign links in App Store Connect Analytics > Acquisition > Campaigns. Recommended campaign tokens:
- msextra
- facebook
- garagebuilt
- tiktok
- youtube
- cruxwebsite

Once the first Apple campaign link is created, reuse the same pt (provider token) and mt values; change only ct for each campaign. Example format from Apple:
https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6778061607?pt=PROVIDER_TOKEN&ct=msextra&mt=8

Do not invent the provider token. Copy it from App Store Connect.
